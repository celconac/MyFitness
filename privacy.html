MyFitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. About MyFitness

MyFitness is a fitness tracking application that helps users record workouts, exercise routines, food intake, calories, protein and body measurements.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the application, MyFitness may process your Google account name, email address, profile picture, fitness records, food logs, workout details and body measurements.

3. Google Account and Drive Access

MyFitness uses Google authentication and may request permission to access files created or selected for use with MyFitness in your Google Drive. This access is used to save, retrieve and update your fitness records.

MyFitness does not require unrestricted access to all files in your Google Drive.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

The intended storage system keeps each user's MyFitness data in their own Google Drive account. Fitness records are not intended to be publicly accessible or shared with other users. Data handling is subject to the permissions and storage configuration implemented in the application.

5. Use of Google User Data

Google user data is used to provide authentication and the fitness tracking functions requested by the user. It is not sold or used for advertising. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can request assistance locating or deleting their MyFitness data by contacting the developer. Removing MyFitness access from Google Account settings revokes future access but does not automatically delete previously stored fitness records.

7. Security

MyFitness is intended to use Google OAuth and user-specific access permissions. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when MyFitness features or data handling practices change. The latest version will appear on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries or data deletion requests, email celcon.ac@gmail.com.
